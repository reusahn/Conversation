# Earlier project decisions

This is a reconstructed summary from the visible working context and existing planning documents, not a verbatim transcript of every earlier conversation.

- The project is a viewer tool for sharing a viral clip with source evidence. Viral means widely circulated; it does not mean false, edited or AI generated.
- The user chose to keep video and image analysis. The guide lists Simple Image Analyzers, not a confirmed blanket ban of visual processing. This does not establish organizer permission for ContextLens.
- Initial flow: allowed clip and caption, actual source interval matching within a disclosed collection, playable comparison and manual sharing.
- Native Instagram home feed reading/filtering is not assumed. DM entry and arbitrary video/comment access are unimplemented and outside the first release.
- Optional comments identify explicit expressions or questions within a bounded authorized sample. They do not measure private beliefs or population gullibility.
- Release, filming, event and repost dates are distinct. Unknown source or absent AI records do not establish fakery. Source attribution does not establish copyright permission.
